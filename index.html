What the model accounts for

The costs that rent-vs-buy calculators usually leave out are where the answer often flips.

Mortgage amortization

Every payment is split into interest and principal, month by month, so equity builds the way it really does.

Service charges & property tax

HOA or service fees with their own escalation rate, plus annual property tax on the home's value.

Marginal tax shields

Optional deductions for mortgage interest and property tax at your marginal bracket.

Rent escalation

Rent grows every year at a rate you set, so the renter's cost isn't frozen at today's price.

Opportunity cost of capital

The renter invests the down payment and any monthly savings at your expected market return.

Transaction costs

Toggle buying closing costs and selling costs to see what it takes to actually exit at year ten.
